Creative Philosophy

Meaningful rooms are built from meaningful things.

i.

Art gives a room its voice

The work on the walls says who lives there. It should be chosen — or made — with the same care as the people it belongs to.

ii.

Framing is the bridge

A well-chosen frame connects the artwork to the room around it, protecting what matters while letting it be seen at its best.

iii.

Space is the setting

Light, scale, colour and proportion decide how a piece is experienced. Design the room and the art finally has somewhere to belong.

Approach

Every project is treated as a whole: the artwork, the way it is presented and the room it lives in. Rather than working on each in isolation, the studio considers how they inform one another — so that a commission is painted with its frame in mind, and a frame is chosen with its wall in mind.

The result is work that feels resolved and personal, rather than assembled.
